Wellington County Farms

Serving Wellington County's
Dairy, Grain & Beef Farms

Wellington County's north townships — Mount Forest, Arthur, Harriston, and Palmerston — are among the most active dairy and beef cattle farming communities in Ontario. The county also produces significant volumes of grain and cash crops, with large operations throughout Centre Wellington, Mapleton, and Minto. Farms at this scale need structures that can cover wide equipment, store large volumes, and perform reliably through Ontario winters.

MegaDome® fabric buildings offer Wellington County farms clear-span widths from 20 to 200 feet, with no interior columns to interfere with equipment movement or livestock management. CoverIT Canada engineers every structure to Wellington County's snow and wind requirements, with stamped drawings for County of Wellington and all local township building permits.

We work throughout the county and are familiar with the permit process across Wellington's many municipalities — from Centre Wellington in the south to Minto and Mapleton in the north. Our own crew handles every installation from first stake to final walkthrough.
