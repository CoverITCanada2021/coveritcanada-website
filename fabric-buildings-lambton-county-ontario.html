Lambton County Farms

Serving Lambton County's
Grain & Cash Crop Farms

Lambton County's flat, clay-based farmland supports significant corn, soybean, and winter wheat production across its rural townships. Farms around Forest, Wyoming, Petrolia, Watford, and Arkona regularly need additional covered space — for equipment, grain, inputs, and general farm use. MegaDome® fabric buildings deliver that space faster and at lower cost than conventional construction.

Lambton County's position on Lake Huron's eastern shore means many farms in the county's western townships face elevated wind exposure. CoverIT Canada engineers every fabric building to the site-specific wind and snow data, ensuring your structure is properly rated for its location — whether you're in a sheltered inland lot or on exposed ground near the lake.

We provide stamped engineering drawings for all County of Lambton and local municipality permit applications, and our own crew handles every installation from start to finish.
