MegaDome® Buildings
Installed Across Ontario

CoverIT Canada has completed 200+ fabric building installations across Ontario since 2009 — from small farm equipment sheds to large municipal salt domes and remote mine site structures. Every building in this gallery was installed by our own crew.

Projects include agricultural buildings, municipal public works structures, industrial warehouses, equestrian arenas, and commercial facilities. We work in every region of Ontario — from the GTA and Southwestern Ontario to Northern Ontario and Northwestern Ontario mine sites.

  • Agricultural: Livestock barns, grain storage, hay buildings, equestrian arenas, equipment sheds
  • Municipal: Salt and sand storage domes, public works equipment shelters, road maintenance facilities
  • Mining: Equipment storage, maintenance shops, bulk material handling at remote Ontario mine sites
  • Commercial & Industrial: Warehouses, contractor yards, utilities storage, indoor recreation facilities
