Oxford County Farms

Built for Oxford County's
Dairy & Grain Operations

Oxford County is one of Ontario's premier dairy regions — and one of the province's most active markets for agricultural fabric buildings. Farms throughout Woodstock, Ingersoll, Norwich, Tavistock, Thamesford, and Embro need reliable covered storage for equipment, grain, hay, and supplies. A well-engineered MegaDome® structure delivers that space quickly and at a fraction of the cost of conventional construction.

CoverIT Canada has installed fabric buildings on dairy and cash-crop farms across Oxford County. We understand the specific demands of the region — the equipment that needs to be kept dry through the winter, the grain that needs to be protected between harvest and sale, and the snow loads that Oxford County structures must be designed to carry.

Every MegaDome® we install in Oxford County is engineered with stamped drawings suitable for county building permits. We handle the full process — from site assessment and sizing through to final installation — with our own crew.
