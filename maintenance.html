A fabric building is a long-term investment. MegaDome® structures are engineered for 25+ years of service and the PowerShield® membrane is rated for 15–20 years — but only when the building receives the care it needs.

Inspection Services

What Our Maintenance
Visit Covers

Every maintenance visit includes a systematic check of all critical building components. We follow the MegaDome® owner’s schedule as a baseline and expand based on building age and condition.

01
Foundation

Visual inspection of all foundation types — concrete blocks, grade beams, perimeter walls, and helical anchors. We check for cracks, settlement, and block alignment.

02
Frame & Structure

We check for untied braces, bent purlins, warped or damaged arch sections, and any slack steel cables. These are the most common wear points on aging buildings.

03
Membrane & Lacing

Integrity check of the entire membrane — tarp turnbuckle tension, safety bolts, and any wear or damage along the lacing system. Early detection prevents panel failure.

04
Doors & Ventilation

Lubrication of all door mechanisms per manufacturer specs. Ventilation devices checked to confirm they’re functioning and providing adequate airflow.

Ontario Conditions

Snow, Wind & Winter
Management

Ontario winters put fabric buildings under significant stress. Snow accumulation on the roof and sides is the most serious concern — and monitoring it is the building owner’s responsibility after every major storm.

MegaDome® buildings are engineered for Ontario snow loads, but they must be cleared when accumulation approaches recommended limits. We recommend all owners maintain a log of post-storm inspections and any clearing performed.

Wind management is equally important. All building openings — doors and ventilation systems — should be closed when wind speed exceeds 50 km/h. Leaving openings exposed in high wind creates uplift forces that the building is not designed to resist.
