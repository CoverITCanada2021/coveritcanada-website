Haldimand County Farms

Serving Haldimand County's
Grain & Cash Crop Operations

Haldimand County's fertile Grand River valley and Lake Erie plain support large-scale grain and cash crop production. Farms throughout Cayuga, Hagersville, Dunnville, and Jarvis produce corn, soybeans, winter wheat, and other cash crops, with equipment fleets and storage needs that match the scale of their operations. MegaDome® fabric buildings are an efficient, cost-effective way to add covered space for grain, equipment, and farm inputs.

Haldimand County's Lake Erie proximity means farms in the county's southern and western portions can experience significant wind. CoverIT Canada engineers every MegaDome® installation to Haldimand County's site-specific wind and snow data, ensuring your structure is properly rated regardless of its location within the county. Every project includes stamped engineering drawings for Haldimand County building permit applications.

We serve Haldimand County from our Southwestern Ontario reach, regularly working in the county and surrounding areas. Our own crew handles every installation from start to finish.
