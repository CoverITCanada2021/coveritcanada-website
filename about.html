15+ Years

CoverIT Canada has been designing and installing fabric buildings across Ontario for over 15 years. We work with farmers, municipalities, mining operations, and commercial operators who need durable, cost-effective structures that hold up through harsh Canadian winters, heavy snow loads, and everything in between.

We are based in Thornhill, Ontario with our operations yard in Peterborough — which means we are well-positioned to serve clients across the province, from the GTA to Northern Ontario.

As an authorized MegaDome dealer, we carry one of the most trusted lines of fabric buildings in Canada, backed by 50+ years of Canadian manufacturing by Industries Harnois.
