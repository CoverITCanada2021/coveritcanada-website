How It Works
The Re-cover
Process

We start with an on-site assessment so we can accurately measure your structure, evaluate the frame condition, and give you a precise quote — not a ballpark figure over the phone.

Our site visit fee is $1,500, which covers travel, full measurement, and a written assessment of your building. If you proceed with a re-cover, the $1,500 is credited in full toward the cost of your new fabric cover.

Once approved, we schedule the work around your operation. Our crew handles everything — fabric removal, frame inspection, new cover installation, and a final walkthrough.
