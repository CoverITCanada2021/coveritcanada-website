Ottawa & Region

Serving Ottawa and
Eastern Ontario

Ottawa and the surrounding region is a significant market for fabric buildings. The City of Ottawa and surrounding townships operate large public works fleets requiring salt and sand storage, equipment shelters, and maintenance facilities. CoverIT Canada has completed multiple municipal fabric building projects in Eastern Ontario and understands the procurement and permitting requirements involved.

Eastern Ontario also has strong agricultural activity — grain and hay storage, livestock facilities, and equipment barns are common projects throughout Renfrew County, Lanark County, and the Ottawa Valley. We work with farmers across the region to specify the right MegaDome® building for their site and budget.

No project is too remote. Whether you are in downtown Ottawa, Carleton Place, Smiths Falls, or deep in the Ottawa Valley, CoverIT Canada will come to your site and get the job done right.
