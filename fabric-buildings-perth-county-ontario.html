Perth County Farms

Built for Perth County's
Hog, Dairy & Grain Operations

Perth County is one of the most intensively farmed counties in all of Canada. Its hog and pork production, dairy operations, and large-scale grain farming create steady demand for cost-effective covered storage and farm structures. MegaDome® fabric buildings are widely used throughout the county for grain and fertilizer storage, equipment protection, and general farm use.

Farms around Listowel, Milverton, Mitchell, and Monkton operate at significant scale — and need structures that can be sized to match. MegaDome® buildings are available in widths from 20 to 200 feet and in any length, making it straightforward to design around your existing layout, grain handling equipment, or vehicle clearance requirements.

CoverIT Canada manages every Perth County project with our own crew, from the initial site visit through to the final inspection. We know the county's permit process and provide sealed engineering drawings as part of every contract.
