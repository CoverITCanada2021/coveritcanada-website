Huron County Farms

Engineered for Huron County's
Grain & Livestock Farms

Huron County generates more farm cash receipts than almost any other inland county in Ontario. The region's corn, soybean, and winter wheat operations — combined with its significant hog and beef production — create constant demand for reliable, large-span covered storage. MegaDome® fabric buildings are a proven solution for Huron County farms that need to protect grain, equipment, and inputs without the cost and timeline of conventional steel or wood construction.

One factor unique to Huron County is wind exposure. Located adjacent to Lake Huron, farms throughout the county — particularly west of Goderich and along the lakeshore — experience higher sustained wind loads than most of inland Ontario. Every MegaDome® structure CoverIT Canada installs in Huron County is engineered to meet the wind and snow requirements for your specific site, not a generic provincial average.

We provide stamped engineering drawings for all County of Huron and local municipality building permits, and our own crew handles every installation from foundation preparation through final fabric tensioning.
