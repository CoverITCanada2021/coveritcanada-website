Thunder Bay & Region

Northwestern Ontario's
Fabric Building Specialists

Thunder Bay and Northwestern Ontario present some of the most demanding building conditions in the province — extreme cold, heavy snow, and remote project sites. CoverIT Canada has experience working in exactly these environments. MegaDome® structures are built for it, and our installation crew is equipped and experienced for Northern Ontario deployments.

The Northwestern Ontario mining sector — from Thunder Bay through Red Lake, Kenora, and Dryden — is a major application for fabric buildings. Mine sites need equipment storage, maintenance shops, and bulk material handling facilities that can be erected quickly and hold up for years in remote conditions. We have completed multiple mine site projects and understand the unique requirements of operating in Northern Ontario's resource sector.

The City of Thunder Bay and surrounding First Nations and municipalities also regularly require salt domes, equipment shelters, and public works storage. CoverIT Canada delivers projects on time and on budget, with the sealed engineering documentation required for municipal procurement.
