MegaDome® Series
Profiles & Dimensions

Each MegaDome® series is available in multiple profiles — different arch diameters and heights to match your clearance, equipment, and snow load requirements. All profiles use galvanized double-arch steel and carry sealed Ontario engineering.

AR Series
Gable & Permanent Buildings — Custom Engineered

The AR Series uses a 30° gable pitch roof and conventional-style framing. Widths from 60' to 100', eave heights 10'–16', ridge heights 30'–45'. Every AR building is custom-engineered to your site requirements. Contact us to discuss your project.
