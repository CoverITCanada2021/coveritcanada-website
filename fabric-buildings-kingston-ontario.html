Kingston & Area

Serving Kingston and
Frontenac County

Kingston sits at the junction of Eastern and Central Ontario and is well within our regular service territory. CoverIT Canada's Peterborough operations yard puts us within easy driving distance of Kingston and the surrounding region — Frontenac County, Lennox & Addington, Prince Edward County, and the Quinte area.

The Kingston region has a diverse mix of agricultural, commercial, and institutional projects that are a strong fit for fabric buildings. Municipal salt and sand storage is a consistent requirement across Kingston and its surrounding townships. The strong agricultural base in Prince Edward County and Frontenac County generates ongoing demand for farm storage and equipment shelters.

We have completed projects in the Kingston area across multiple industries and understand the local permit requirements and soil conditions throughout the region.
