Renfrew County Farms

Built for Renfrew County's
Beef Cattle & Mixed Farms

Renfrew County stretches from the Ottawa Valley to the edge of Algonquin Park and contains some of Eastern Ontario's most productive beef cattle and mixed farming operations. Farms throughout the county — from Arnprior and Cobden in the south to Eganville, Barry's Bay, and Killaloe in the north — need robust, weather-tight structures that can handle Renfrew County's significant winter snowfall and cold temperatures.

MegaDome® fabric buildings are well suited to Renfrew County's conditions. Their arched steel structure sheds snow naturally, reducing the risk of snow accumulation that can stress a conventional flat or low-pitch roof. CoverIT Canada engineers every building to Renfrew County's actual snow and wind data, with stamped drawings from a licensed Ontario engineer included with every contract.

We serve Renfrew County regularly from our Ottawa-area reach, and our crew is experienced with the terrain, access conditions, and permit processes that apply throughout the county and its many local townships.
