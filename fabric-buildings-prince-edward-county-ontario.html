Prince Edward County Farms

Serving Prince Edward County's
Grain, Vineyard & Mixed Farms

Prince Edward County is a farming peninsula surrounded by Lake Ontario on three sides. While the county has become well known for its wine and culinary scene, its agricultural base remains strong — grain, cash crop, beef, and mixed farming operations throughout Hillier, Ameliasburgh, North Marysburgh, and South Marysburgh continue to need functional, cost-effective farm structures.

The county's exposure to Lake Ontario creates meaningful wind loads that must be factored into any permanent structure. CoverIT Canada engineers every MegaDome® installation in Prince Edward County to site-specific wind and snow data, providing stamped drawings that meet the County of Prince Edward's building permit requirements.

We're based in Peterborough and regularly work throughout Eastern Ontario, making Prince Edward County a straightforward service area for our crew. Whether you need grain storage near Wellington, equipment protection near Bloomfield, or a general farm structure near Picton, we can help.
