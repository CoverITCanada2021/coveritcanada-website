Bruce County Farms

Built for Bruce County's
Dairy, Beef & Grain Farms

Bruce County has one of the highest concentrations of dairy and beef cattle farms in Ontario. The county's farms — from Teeswater and Mildmay in the south to Wiarton and Tobermory in the north — operate in challenging winter conditions. Bruce County experiences significant lake-effect snowfall from both Georgian Bay and Lake Huron, making proper snow load engineering critical for any permanent farm structure.

MegaDome® fabric buildings installed by CoverIT Canada are engineered to Bruce County's specific snow and wind loads, not generic provincial values. Every project includes stamped drawings from a licensed Ontario engineer and is fully supported for Bruce County and local municipality building permit applications. We also factor in the county's notable wind exposure — particularly important for farms on the Bruce Peninsula and along the lakeshore.

Whether you're a dairy producer in Walkerton needing a heifer shelter, a beef operation near Chesley looking for feed storage, or a grain farmer near Teeswater needing equipment space, CoverIT Canada can recommend the right structure and install it properly.
