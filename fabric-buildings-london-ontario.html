London & Southwestern Ontario

Serving London and
Southwestern Ontario

Southwestern Ontario is one of the most agriculturally productive regions in Canada and one of our most active service areas. Middlesex County, Elgin County, Oxford County, and Huron County have dense farm populations with significant demand for grain and equipment storage, livestock facilities, and equestrian buildings. CoverIT Canada has installed fabric buildings on farms across this entire region.

London also has a large industrial and commercial base — manufacturing facilities, contractor operations, and distribution centres that benefit from clear-span fabric buildings for covered storage and work areas. MegaDome® structures are cost-effective and fast to install compared to conventional construction, making them ideal for Southwestern Ontario's busy commercial sector.

We regularly travel to the London area and have completed projects in Middlesex, Elgin, Oxford, Huron, and Perth counties. Our crew is experienced and equipped to handle projects anywhere in Southwestern Ontario.
