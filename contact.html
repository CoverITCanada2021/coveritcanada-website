How It Works

What to Expect
When You Reach Out

Getting a quote from CoverIT Canada is straightforward. You reach out, we ask a few questions about your site, intended use, and size requirements, and we give you a clear, honest answer — no pressure and no vague estimates that change later.

Most fabric building quotes can be turned around within one business day. For larger or more complex projects — multi-span structures, municipal contracts, or mine site deployments — we'll schedule a site visit or a call to make sure we understand exactly what you need before putting numbers together.

We've been doing this for over 15 years. We know what works in Ontario's climate, what the permit process looks like in different municipalities, and what questions you should be asking before you commit to a structure. Whether you're a farmer in Huron County, a municipality in Northern Ontario, or a contractor in the GTA, we'll give you the same straight answer.
