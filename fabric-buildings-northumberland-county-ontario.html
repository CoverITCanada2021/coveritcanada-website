Northumberland County Farms

Serving Northumberland County's
Grain & Mixed Farm Operations

Northumberland County sits between the densely farmed lands of the GTA and the mixed farming regions of Eastern Ontario. The county produces corn, soybeans, grain, and a variety of cash crops, with some fruit and apple orchards along the Lake Ontario shoreline. Farms in the county's interior — around Campbellford, Warkworth, and Hastings — operate on productive land with real needs for covered storage and equipment protection.

CoverIT Canada's Peterborough operations yard puts us in a strong position to serve Northumberland County quickly. We regularly install and service fabric buildings throughout the county and are familiar with the local permit process for Northumberland County and its constituent municipalities.

Every MegaDome® we install is engineered for the site's specific snow and wind conditions, backed by stamped drawings from a licensed Ontario engineer, and installed by our own crew from start to finish.
