Greater Sudbury sits at the heart of Northern Ontario's mining corridor. CoverIT Canada has extensive experience installing fabric buildings in this region — from equipment maintenance shops and bulk material storage facilities at active mine sites, to municipal public works facilities and agricultural structures in the surrounding Nickel Belt communities.

Northern Ontario's snow loads and wind conditions demand structures that are specifically engineered for the site. MegaDome® buildings are Canadian-engineered and have a proven track record in exactly these conditions. Every project we install in Sudbury includes site-specific sealed engineering drawings stamped for local building permits.

We have completed projects across Northern Ontario and are not deterred by remote locations or challenging site access. Our crew mobilizes from our Peterborough operations yard and is equipped to handle the full scope of installation work in any Northern Ontario environment.
