CoverIT Canada's operations yard is based in Peterborough — which means you get the fastest response times and lowest mobilization costs of any Ontario fabric building supplier. We have been serving the Peterborough, Kawartha Lakes, and Northumberland area for over 15 years and have completed more projects in this region than any other part of the province.

The Peterborough and Kawartha region has a strong agricultural base — dairy operations, horse facilities, grain farms, and cash crop operations throughout Peterborough County, Havelock, Norwood, Warsaw, and Omemee. We have installed dozens of MegaDome® structures on farms throughout this area and know exactly what works here.

We also serve municipalities across the Kawartha region — including the City of Peterborough, City of Kawartha Lakes, Northumberland County, and surrounding townships — with salt domes, equipment storage, and public works facilities.
